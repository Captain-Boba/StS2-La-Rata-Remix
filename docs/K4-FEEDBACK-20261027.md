Variables: `Dose`, native-localized `Debuff`; Sturdy uses `Amount`. Doomweaver is a legacy fallback, not newly selectable.

The marked move remains the existing deterministic choice: compatible attack/buff/defend/debuff moves, nonattacks first, then ordinal move ID. Effects repeat AFTER that exact move finishes and require the source to be alive and combat not ending. They affect its living player targets; neither a hit nor HP loss is required. Their new tooltips identify that move. No compatible move produces an explicit inactive explanation and warning; Echoing's separate short-debuff amplifier still applies.

No new arm/disarm or dispel contract: removing a presentation marker does not disable an encounter rule. Supported-source caller audit found no arbitrary positive enemy-power removal: native Expose removes Artifact only; La Rata removes Thorns, Vulnerable, Debuffs, Weak/Frail or its own intervention. Rend counts Debuffs only. Generic removal paths are death, escape/reset, player lifecycle and debug. Manual removal or another mod removing arbitrary Buffs can remove the marker while the rule/intent persists; this is a bounded compatibility limitation, not grounds for a remove blocker, regrant loop or alternate UI system.

Concrete exception checked: native `ToughEgg.HatchMove` clears all powers except Minion. ToughEgg is a later Ovicopter summon, not in `OvicopterNormal.GenerateMonsters()` (initial roster contains only Ovicopter), and is absent from the La Rata reinforcement allowlist. Therefore it receives no director twist/Hex marker in the supported path. `PlowPower` removes only temporary Strength and Strength; AutoSlay's broad removal is a debug handler. No supported living twist recipient losing this rule marker through those paths was found.

## Recommended damage ramp — DESIGN ONLY

One recommendation: apply 40%, 50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 90%, then 100% from round 7 to powered attacks from RatKing and the two Kingsguards **only inside RatKingEncounter**. Standalone Kingsguard encounters, normal interventions and player cards remain outside scope. Existing card-first/monster-action ordering remains intact.

Reason: leaving guard attacks untouched would retain Headsman 20x2 plus Herald 10x2 = 60 opening AoE damage per player even with a softened boss. The ramp ceiling is 100% of today's attack calculation, not an absolute damage cap: native Strength/Ritual growth can still increase later attacks.

Current attack bases/hits: Royal IDs 0=12, 1=7x3, 2=34, 9=5x4, 10=18, 11=6x3, 17=40, 18=4x5, 23=26, 29=3x4, 37=4, 40=5x2, all AoE. No attack card gives a fallback attack of 12. Boss energy is 6, then 8 at half HP; draw/energy relics and next-hand banks influence the selected hand. Strength sources: Vajra +1 boss, Blood Contract +2 court, Kneel +3 court, native Ritual growth (Ritual cap 6). Vigor: 4 from adapters 27/39/42 and 3 from Smuggler, for the first attack. Vulnerable, Artifact, Cleanse/Pardon and Graveyard consumption are already forecasted.

Guard cycles: Bulwark support / 18x2 / 36; Headsman 20x2 / support / 36; Armorer support / 16 / heal; Herald 10x2 / heal / support. Two of four are selected at encounter creation.

Nonattack pressure remains unchanged in this recommendation: Mercury Hourglass 3 AoE each enemy-side start; Letter Opener 5 AoE after 3 Skills, at most once/hand; Thorns 3; Poison from cards/Court; Doom; status cards/Burn, Weak/Frail and Black Tithe removing Block. Poison ticks have no original dealer reference; Doom is an execution check, not attack damage. No silent global scaling of these paths is proposed. Consequently this is not a guarantee of a fixed maximum total HP loss.

Apply the factor to each hit after native additive Strength/Vigor and before final native caps/truncation. Keep Weak/Vulnerable/Cruelty multipliers native. Share the native Hook.ModifyDamage path with RoyalForecastAttackIntent; do not pre-round card bases or add a second preview multiplier. Use the native combat round/lifecycle, not elapsed time or number of cards. Native RoundNumber increments at the ordinary enemy-to-player switch, not player extra turns; preview timing must be checked against the announced move.
