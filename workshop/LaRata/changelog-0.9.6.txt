[h1]La Rata Remix – Version 0.9.6[/h1]

[h2]EN – English[/h2]
[b]1. New Content[/b]
[list]
[*]11 new La Rata cards.
[*]10 new encounter twists and 8 additional reinforcement types.
[/list]

[b]New relics[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Counterfeit Seal[/b]: The first card you create each turn is upgraded, if possible.
[*][b]Plague Testament[/b]: When an enemy dies, transfer its Poison to a random enemy.
[*][b]Usurper's Tooth[/b]: Whenever an enemy gains Strength, gain 2 Vigor.
[/list]

[b]New potions[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Brass Polish[/b]: Gain Vigor equal to your Block.
[*][b]Grave Distillate[/b]: Swap an enemy’s Poison and Doom.
[*][b]Amber Hourglass[/b]: Block is not removed at the start of your next 2 turns.
[/list]

[b]2. Balancing[/b]
[list]
[*]La Rata now has 5/7 base Energy and draws 10 combat relics from 25 options. Lantern and Happy Flower are replaced.
[*]Withering affects only the next starting hand and does not stack. Reinforcements repeat less often.
[*]Reworked Sootbound Ticket and Stairwell Key; removed Brass Scavenger’s Gold cap. Relic Remover is now Uncommon.
[*]Adjusted several cards and the boss deck. Reset costs 2/1 Energy, always Exhausts and refunds its own paid cost. Rustwater also removes Plating.
[/list]

[b]3. Bug Fixes[/b]
[list]
[*]Fixed Reset’s co-op energy accounting. Added royal damage-ramp indicators and a Well contest animation.
[*]Updated artwork and concise tooltips. Playable boss cards use a red rarity banner; their La Rata booster pool was verified.
[/list]

[b]4. Compatibility / Known Issues[/b]
[list]
[*]Requires Public v0.107.1 and BaseLib 3.4.7; the game Beta branch is unsupported. Start a new run with matching versions in co-op.
[*]Relic statistics remain disabled. Broader multiplayer and balance validation is ongoing.
[/list]


[h2]CN – 简体中文[/h2]
[b]1. 新内容[/b]
[list]
[*]新增11张 La Rata 卡牌。
[*]新增10种遭遇变数和8种援军怪物。
[/list]

[b]新遗物[/b]
[list]
[*][b]伪造印玺[/b]: 每回合你创建的第一张牌会被升级（若能升级）。
[*][b]瘟疫遗嘱[/b]: 当一名敌人死亡时，将其中毒转移至一名随机敌人。
[*][b]篡位者之牙[/b]: 每当敌人获得力量时，你获得2层振奋。
[/list]

[b]新药水[/b]
[list]
[*][b]黄铜抛光剂[/b]: 获得等同于你格挡值的活力。
[*][b]墓地馏液[/b]: 交换一名敌人的中毒与灾厄层数。
[*][b]琥珀沙漏[/b]: 你的接下来2个回合开始时，格挡不会被移除。
[/list]

[b]2. 平衡调整[/b]
[list]
[*]La Rata 的基础能量调整为5/7，每场战斗从25种遗物中选择10件。灯笼与开心小花已被替换。
[*]枯萎仅减少下一回合的起始抽牌数，且不叠加。援军怪物更少重复出现。
[*]重做焦灰车票与楼梯间钥匙；移除黄铜拾荒者的金币上限。遗物移除器调整为罕见。
[*]调整多张卡牌与首领牌组。重置的费用为2/1点能量，两个版本均消耗，并返还自身实际支付的能量。锈水现在也移除覆甲。
[/list]

[b]3. 错误修复[/b]
[list]
[*]修复重置在联机中的能量返还记录。新增皇家敌人的伤害递增标识与井的争夺动画。
[*]更新插画并精简描述。玩家可用的首领卡牌采用红色稀有度横幅，并已验证其在 La Rata 补充包中的卡池。
[/list]

[b]4. 兼容性 / 已知问题[/b]
[list]
[*]需要游戏正式分支（Public）v0.107.1 和 BaseLib 3.4.7，不支持游戏测试分支（Beta）。请开始新的一局，联机玩家须使用相同版本。
[*]遗物统计仍停用。更广泛的多人测试与平衡验证仍在进行。
[/list]
