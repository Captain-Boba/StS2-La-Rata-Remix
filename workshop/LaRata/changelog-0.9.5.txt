[h1]Version 0.9.5 - Fixes & Potions[/h1]

[h2]EN – English[/h2]
[b]1. New Content[/b]
Three new potions:
[list]
[*][b]Ashbrand[/b]: Exhaust a non-X card in your Hand. Gain Energy equal to its cost before temporary discounts.
[*][b]Rustwater[/b]: Remove all Block from an enemy.
[*][b]Crown Lacquer[/b]: Choose a card in your Hand. Remove its Exhaust keyword this combat.
[/list]
[b]2. Balancing[/b]
[list]
[*]La Rata and his two bodyguards now start at 40% attack damage, increasing to 100% by turn 7.
[/list]
[b]3. Bug Fixes[/b]
[list]
[*]Fixed several co-op synchronization and disconnect issues. All relic run statistics are temporarily disabled.
[*]Fixed booster card focus, improved twist status indicators and simplified card text.
[*]Updated card artwork and relic outlines.
[/list]
[b]4. Compatibility / Known Issues[/b]
[list]
[*]Public branch v0.107.1 and BaseLib 3.4.7 required; the game Beta branch is not supported. Start a new run with matching versions for all co-op players.
[*]Further multiplayer stability and balancing work is ongoing.
[/list]

[h2]CN – 简体中文[/h2]
[b]1. 新内容[/b]
三种新药水：
[list]
[*][b]灰烬燃剂[/b]: 消耗一张非X费手牌。获得等同于其临时减费前费用的能量。
[*][b]锈水[/b]: 移除一名敌人的所有格挡。
[*][b]王冠漆[/b]: 选择一张手牌。在本场战斗中移除其消耗关键词。
[/list]
[b]2. 平衡调整[/b]
[list]
[*]La Rata 及其两名护卫的攻击伤害从 40% 开始，逐回合提高，到第 7 回合达到 100%。
[/list]
[b]3. 错误修复[/b]
[list]
[*]修复多项联机同步和断线问题。所有本局遗物统计暂时停用。
[*]修复补充包卡牌焦点问题，改进遭遇变数状态标识，并精简卡牌描述。
[*]更新部分卡牌插画和遗物轮廓。
[/list]
[b]4. 兼容性 / 已知问题[/b]
[list]
[*]需要游戏正式分支（Public）v0.107.1 和 BaseLib 3.4.7；不支持游戏测试分支（Beta）。请开始新的一局，所有联机玩家须使用相同版本。
[*]多人稳定性与平衡调整仍在持续完善。
[/list]
