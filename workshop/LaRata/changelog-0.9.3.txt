[h1]Version 0.9.3 - Double Boss Spoils[/h1]

[h2]Final boss rewards[/h2]
- Restore rewards after the regular final boss, before the La Rata invitation.
- Defeating the reinforced boss duo now gives each player two complete native boss reward batches: gold from both bosses, two independent three-card boss offers and two potion rolls.
- White Beast Statue guarantees both potions. Without a compatible reinforcement, the regular finale gives one boss reward batch.
- Include room-specific extra rewards once. La Rata's own victory and the Architect transition remain unchanged.

[h2]Cards, relics and twists[/h2]
- Move vanilla Barricade from Rare to Uncommon in the Ironclad pool. Its effects, energy costs and upgrade remain unchanged. Bowler Hat retains its native gold effect.
- Reduce the selection weight of Bristling and Toxic Shell by 75%, making Thorns twists substantially less common without changing their strength or the encounter budget.
- Fix A Thousand Cuts removal pricing: each successful removal raises the next price through the normal saved counter. Existing removal history, discounts and restrictions remain in effect; cancelled removals do not increase the price.

[h2]Compatibility and work in progress[/h2]
- Requires Slay the Spire 2 v0.107.1 and BaseLib 3.4.7. All co-op players must use matching versions; finish a shared session before updating and start a new run for revised encounter generation.
- We recommend starting without Ascension levels.
- Balancing and multiplayer compatibility testing remain work in progress. German and Chinese localizations are planned; mod-added text is currently English only.
