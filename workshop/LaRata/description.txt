[b]Current version: 0.9.2[/b]

La Rata Remix brings controlled chaos and tougher, more varied encounters to Slay the Spire 2, scaling from solo to four-player co-op.

[h2]New content[/h2]
[list]
[*]43 encounter twists, extra enemies and a potion-throwing goblin.
[*]An optional endgame path featuring The Kingsguard and La Rata: a boss with his own 23-card deck, ten combat relics and two knight bodyguards.
[*]11 neutral cards, including Cleanse, and 10 playable royal boss-card variants, all with upgrades.
[*]Six animated, seven-card booster packs, five combo relics, Well and A Thousand Cuts. Every player starts with a class booster.
[*]Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors for contested treasure, three relic choices in solo chests, and combat histories for royal cards and goblin potions.
[/list]

[h2]Work in progress[/h2]
Balancing is the main focus: encounter twists, solo and co-op scaling, The Kingsguard and La Rata, and card/relic combinations. Visual polish and multiplayer compatibility testing are ongoing.
