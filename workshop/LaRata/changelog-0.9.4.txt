[h1]La Rata Remix – Version 0.9.4[/h1]

[h2]EN – English[/h2]
[b]1. New Content[/b]
[list]
[*]14 new La Rata Cards.
[*]6 new Relics.
[*]German and Simplified Chinese localization.
[*]Dedicated La Rata card compendium and relic run statistics.
[*]New and reworked artwork, including updated boss animations.
[/list]
[b]2. Balancing[/b]
[list]
[*]Expanded La Rata's boss card and relic pools. Reduced boss healing and direct Block.
[*]Adjusted Act 1 reinforcement costs to allow more varied encounter twists.
[*]Adjusted card rarities and booster drop rates.
[*]Reworked several card effects.
[/list]
[b]3. Bug Fixes[/b]
[list]
[*]A Thousand Cuts now keeps removal costs at the first removal's base price, with applicable price modifiers.
[*]Fixed card cost display and relic tracking issues.
[*]Added visible indicators for enemy HP and damage modifiers.
[*]Simplified card descriptions and relic statistics tooltips.
[/list]
[b]4. Compatibility / Known Issues[/b]
[list]
[*]Requires Slay the Spire 2 v0.107.1 and BaseLib 3.4.7. Start a new run.
[*]All co-op players should use matching game, BaseLib and mod versions.
[*]Closing a local co-op client can produce connection errors on the host. Investigation is ongoing.
[*]Some complex relic effects are not yet tracked by run statistics.
[*]Balance, visual polish and multiplayer testing remain ongoing.
[/list]
